## Contributing

[CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/autowarefoundation/openadkit/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
is the guide: sign-off, branches and pull requests, the local checks and
deployment validation. Discuss a significant architectural change on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/Q94UsPvReQ) before implementing it.

- [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/autowarefoundation/openadkit/issues) — bugs and tasks
- [Autoware Foundation Discord](https://discord.gg/Q94UsPvReQ) — questions, ideas, and design
